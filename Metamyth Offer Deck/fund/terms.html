Metamyth Demand Dividend Note

October 26, 2026 · Zachary Marlow

Metamyth is raising $300K in demand dividend notes, with room to go up to $500K. Investors are repaid from our free cash flow after an 18-month build period, earn the highest return when we grow fastest, and can roll into the commons we are building.

How it works

A demand dividend is repaid from free cash flow, the money left after the business runs, so payments rise and fall with how well the business is actually doing. Nothing is owed until operations generate profit, and there is no sale or exit required for investors to get paid.

The return depends on timing. Investors receive a set multiple of their investment that climbs the longer repayment takes, up to a cap. Repaying fast costs us less in total and earns investors a higher annual return, so both sides want the same thing: more demand, sooner.

Proposed terms

These are draft terms for discussion; definitive terms will be set with counsel.

Term Proposed
Raise $300,000, up to $500,000
Minimum investment Open question
Build period 18 months with no payments, while the capital goes to work
Payments Quarterly, at least 40% of free cash flow, rising to 50% once revenue passes $100K a month. We can pay more at any time.
Free cash flow Cash left after operating costs and an agreed reinvestment budget, so growth stays funded
Return A multiple that rises with time, from 1.4× at year 3 to 2.5× at year 10 (schedule below)
Cap 2.5× the investment
Early repayment Allowed at any time, at the multiple for that date
Conversion Holders may roll their outstanding balance into the next round or the commons

Repayment schedule

The total an investor receives depends on when the note is fully repaid. Between the dates below the multiple rises smoothly; repaying before year 3 costs between 1.2× and 1.4×.

Fully repaid by Multiple Returned per $100K
Year 3 1.4× $140K
Year 5 1.7× $170K
Year 7 2.0× $200K
Year 10 and after 2.5× (cap) $250K

Because payments flow in steadily from the end of the build period, investors start receiving cash years before the note closes. That lifts their effective annual return above what the multiple alone suggests.

What investors can expect

At our Target case, a $300K note is repaid in about 4 years at about 1.5×, an effective return of roughly 16% a year; a full $500K takes just under 5 years at about 1.7×. Our 18-month forecast shows revenue of $712K at Floor, $1.18M at Target and $1.69M at Stretch. The table shows a $300K round.

Case Fully repaid Total returned Effective annual return
Stretch About 2.8 years 1.38× About 16%
Target About 3.8 years 1.51× About 16%
Floor About 5.7 years 1.80× About 16%

These outcomes are preliminary. Revenue and margins beyond the 18-month forecast are assumptions, and a full five-year model driven by demand is being built to replace them.

How it compares

Early-stage equity is slow and usually loses money; this note pays cash from about year two and targets a steady mid-teens return.

Investment Typical outcome When cash comes back
Angel investing About 70% of investments lose money (Angel Resource Institute, via Bankers Anonymous) At an exit, if one comes
Venture funds Median net return of 1.4% a year for 2021 funds and 0.7% for 2022 funds (Carta) About a third of 2021 funds have returned any cash so far
Metamyth demand dividend About 16% a year; repaid in about 4 years at Target, about 6 at Floor Quarterly, starting after the 18-month build period

Venture keeps the possibility of a rare 50–100× winner, which this note caps. The conversion option is how holders reach for that kind of upside here.

What the capital builds

We already have the product and the demand. The capital turns that demand up and builds the system that carries it, so we get profitable at scale.

  • $300K · The Engine: first the team, paid properly while we get the whole operation in shape. Then media: organic marketing, storytelling, brand, a designer and a new website, with no ad spend. Everything else builds the system: a researcher and graph engineer, a second developer, a substrate partnership and an AI-powered workflow. A small reserve sits underneath.
  • Up to $500K · The Ecosystem: more guides and a referral engine running through the guide ecosystem, the capital architecture and legal structure for the commons and the federation, and the events and fundraising that carry the larger vision.

Why this size: getting profitable is the responsible way to grow. We raise only what it takes, then raise more from strength through a blended stack of philanthropy, patient long-term capital and aligned investors. The full use of funds is in the forecast.

The door to the commons

The note is the first step toward a larger instrument: a commons that every venture in the Metamyth ecosystem pays into.

Each venture that comes through the journey will contribute a share of its profit to the collective, stepping up gently as it grows. Investors who have proven to be good partners can convert their note into that commons, holding a stake in the whole ecosystem rather than a single company. Risk is spread and gains compound across many ventures at once, so the upside comes from collective success.

The commons is still being designed and is not part of this offer.

Risks and open terms

Returns are not guaranteed. If growth comes in at Floor, repayment takes about 6 years for a $300K round and about 8 for $500K; if the business fails, investors can lose their capital.
