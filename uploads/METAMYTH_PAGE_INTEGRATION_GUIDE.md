## WHAT KIND OF PAGE THIS IS

**A landing page with one offer.** Its job is to make someone feel it, trust it, and click. The depth moves to two linked pages:

- **`/journey`** · The Journey. The interactive map, both arc scenes, and how the community works.
- **`/expedition`** · The Expedition. One-to-one, for visionaries and ventures already in motion.

The landing page names both and links to both. It never tries to explain everything.

---

## THE EASINESS THREAD

Easiness is the promise, so it threads through the page at rising altitude instead of living in one block. Each appearance goes one level higher, so nothing repeats.

1. **Hero** · Get paid to live your story.

### How the room works

_✦ The work_ · **You work on yourself by working on others.** Turn someone else's wound into a gift three times and your hands know the move before you point it at your own story.

_✦ The room_ · **Nobody is an audience.** Every session rotates roles. You direct, you witness, you receive, and nobody gets to hide.

_✦ The teaching_ · **Experience first, craft second.** You do the thing, the room reflects what happened, and recognizing what you already did is the transformation.
